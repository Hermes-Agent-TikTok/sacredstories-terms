OpenTube

Content scheduling and publishing platform.

OpenTube helps content creators schedule and publish videos to their own TikTok accounts through the official TikTok Content Posting API, with explicit user authorization (OAuth2).

How it works

  • Connect your TikTok account with your explicit consent.
  • Schedule and publish videos to your own account only.
  • No cross-account posting, no automation of other users' accounts.
