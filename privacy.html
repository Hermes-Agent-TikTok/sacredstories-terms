Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

This application connects to social media platforms through their official APIs using account authorization. It accesses only the information required to publish scheduled content.

2. How Information Is Used

Authorization tokens are used exclusively to publish content on behalf of the connected accounts. We do not sell, share, or disclose personal information to third parties.

3. Data Retention

Tokens and account information are stored securely and retained only while the service is in active use.

4. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect account credentials and authorization tokens from unauthorized access.

5. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the channel operator's official contact channels.
